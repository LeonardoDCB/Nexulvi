Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how ArqVello, a product of Nexulvi, handles information when you use the application. ArqVello is designed to organize documents locally and does not require you to create an account.

1. Data handled by the application

ArqVello can access the camera and, when you choose to import or save images, the photo library. These accesses only occur to perform the requested function and depend on the permission of the operating system.

PDF documents, images, file names, categories, dates, favorites and texts recognized by OCR are stored on the device. The OCR of scanned pages is processed on the device itself. ArqVello does not automatically send your library, PDFs, photos, or OCR text to a Nexulvi server.

2. Notifications

If you enable a due date reminder, the app may ask for permission to create a local notification. The reminder is scheduled on the device and can be deactivated or removed by the user.

3. Biometrics

On supported devices, ArqVello can use biometric system authentication to secure application access. The Nexulvi does not receive or store your fingerprint, face or device secret. System biometrics are not file encryption.

4. Export, backup and sharing

Exporting a file, creating a ZIP backup, or sharing a document is an action you initiate. When choosing email, WhatsApp, printing, storage or another application, the file becomes subject to the practices of that destination service. ArqVello does not control what you send to third parties.

Backups can contain documents, images, and library metadata. Check the recipient before sharing them.

5. Third-party ads and services

The Android version of ArqVello may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and protect advertising, the Google SDK may process information such as IP address, device information, advertising identifier, technical data, ad events, and interaction in accordance with Google policies and settings available for your region.

ArqVello configures non-personalized ad requests and does not send documents, images, or OCR text to AdMob to serve ads. Non-personalized requests do not eliminate the SDK's technical treatments or applicable consent and transparency obligations.

See also Google Privacy Policy and your privacy information Google Mobile Ads.

6. What the Nexulvi doesn't do

In the current version, ArqVello does not have registration, login, cloud synchronization, its own analytics or its own server to store its library. Nexulvi does not sell documents, photos, OCR texts or personal data of users.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you delete it through the application, delete exported files, or uninstall ArqVello. The operating system may keep data in device backups according to its own settings. Files already shared with other services need to be deleted on those services by the user.

Since ArqVello does not create accounts, there is no Nexulvi online account to delete. For questions about data or privacy, please contact us by email magalhaesbonfim6@gmail.com.

8. Children

ArqVello is not directed to children and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the application, its suppliers, or applicable laws change. The current version will be published on this page with the update date.
