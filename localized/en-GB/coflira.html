Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how Coflira, a product of Nexulvi, handles information when you use the application. The Coflira was designed for personal financial organization, works without registration and stores entries on the device.

1. Data handled by the application

You can enter descriptions, values, dates, categories, observations, goals, recurrences and financial simulations into the Coflira. This data is stored locally in the application's database, on your device.

In the current version, Coflira does not connect bank accounts, does not request card data, does not have a login, does not maintain cloud synchronization and does not automatically send your financial entries to a Nexulvi server.

2. Third-Party Ads and Services

The Android version of Coflira may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and protect advertising, the Google SDK may process information such as IP address, device information, advertising identifier, technical data, ad events, and interaction in accordance with Google policies and settings available for your region.

Coflira requests non-personalized ads in the current implementation. This does not eliminate the SDK's technical treatments or applicable transparency and consent obligations. Coflira does not send descriptions, values, categories, or financial notes to AdMob to select ads.

See also Google Privacy Policy and your privacy information Google Mobile Ads.

3. Export and sharing

When you generate or share a PDF report, this action is initiated by you. The file becomes subject to the practices of the application or service chosen for sharing. Nexulvi does not control what you send to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Financial data remains on the device until you delete it in the app, clear the app data in system settings, or uninstall Coflira. Since Coflira does not create online accounts, there is no Nexulvi account to delete. The future version of the application should offer an explicit option to erase all local data.

5. Security

Coflira uses the app's private storage and does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. No local system guarantees protection against access to an unlocked device; keep the operating system protected and do not share backups with unauthorized people.

6. Children

Coflira is not directed to children and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application, its suppliers, or applicable laws change. The current version will be published on this page with the update date.
