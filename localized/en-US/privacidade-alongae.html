Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Alongaê is a wellness and ergonomics app. It does not make a diagnosis, does not replace medical advice and should not be used in emergency situations.

1. About us

Alongaê is a product of Nexulvi. For questions about this policy or data processing, please contact us by email contato@alongae.com.br.

2. Data stored on the device

Without registration and without a user account, the application stores settings, preferences, history of breaks and stretches, optional pain records, preference memory and temporary timer data locally on your device. This data is used to perform application functions and is not sent to a Nexulvi server.

3. Ads and Third Parties

The free version of Alongaê displays banners provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process technical information, advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize advertising depending on applicable settings and user choice.

The application uses Google's consent mechanism to present and update privacy choices when required. Ad preferences can be reviewed in Settings → Ad Privacy. Please also see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. User-initiated export and sharing

The application allows you to create backups, reports and CSV files. When you choose to export or share a file, the content is forwarded to the application or service you select in the operating system. This action is initiated by you; review the recipient before confirming.

5. Notifications and permissions

With your permission, Alongaê schedules local notifications for reminders and sessions. On Android, reminders use non-exact alarms and can be snoozed by the system. The application may request permissions for notifications, vibration and operation of an active session in the foreground, depending on the functionality chosen. These permissions can be revoked in system settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you erase it in the app, clear the app storage, uninstall the app, or reset the device. Backups and exported files are under your control at the location where they were sent and must be deleted by you. As there is no account or server of its own, Nexulvi does not maintain a copy of this local data.

7. Security

The application limits storage to what is necessary to function locally. Third-party services, including Google AdMob, have their own security and privacy practices and policies. Don't submit information you don't want shared through ads or an export.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when there are changes to the application, the services used or legal requirements. The update date will change on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or comments: contato@alongae.com.br.
