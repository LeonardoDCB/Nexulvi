Miauforia is an entertainment tool for adult owners and their cats. It is not a children's product, veterinary service or monitoring device.

Miauforia is a product of Nexulvi. For questions about this policy or data, please contact us by email leonardodacostabonfim@gmail.com.

Without registration, the Miauforia locally stores settings, cat profiles, audio preferences, session history, challenges and feedback. This data is necessary for the application's functions and is not sent to Nexulvi's own server.

Google and its partners may process technical information, advertising identifiers, and ad interactions to provide, measure, limit or personalize advertising in accordance with the controller's choices and applicable rules.

The application uses the applicable consent mechanism before requesting ads. Ads policy and preferences can be opened directly from the home screen. See also Google Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

With permission, Miauforia schedules local reminders for fun. Permission can be revoked in system settings. Reminders do not require an account or data submission to Nexulvi.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until erased in the app, system storage, by uninstalling or restoring the device. As there is no account or server for this data, Nexulvi does not keep a copy of it.
